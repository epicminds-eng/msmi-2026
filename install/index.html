Add it to your home screen

📱 iPhone

Open this link in Safari — not Chrome, and not straight from a text or email. That's the #1 thing that goes wrong.

  1. Tap Share — the square with an arrow pointing up. (On newer iPhones, tap the menu button first, then Share.)
  2. Tap Add to Home Screen. Don't see it in the list? Scroll to the bottom, tap Edit Actions , and add it from there.
  3. Make sure Open as Web App is turned on — that's what makes it work without a signal.
  4. Tap Add.

🤖 Android

  1. Open this link in Chrome.
  2. Tap the menu, then Install app — or Add to Home screen, depending on your Chrome version.
  3. Tap Install (or Add).

💻 Older phone, or if that didn't work

No problem — it's just a normal website. Bookmark this link and open it in your browser. Everything works the same, except it won't work without a signal.

  • ·Once it's on your home screen, it works with no signal — that's the whole reason to install it, and it matters at Treetops.
  • ·Tapped the wrong name by mistake? Tap your name at the top of the app to switch.
