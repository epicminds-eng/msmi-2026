One-time setup

Add it to your home screen

📱 iPhone

  1. Open this page in Safari. It won't work from Chrome or from a link inside another app — if you got here from a text or email, tap the address bar, copy the link, and paste it into Safari.
  2. Find the menu:
    • Newer iPhones — tap the three dots (⋯) next to the address bar.
    • Older iPhones — tap the Share button, the square with an arrow pointing up, in the bar at the bottom.
    If you only see one of those, that's yours.
  3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen." You may need to tap "Share" first inside the three-dot menu.
  4. Tap Add. The icon appears on your home screen.

🤖 Android

  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap "Install app" when it appears, or the ⋮ menu then "Add to Home screen."
  3. Tap Install.
Open it from the home screen icon, not from Safari — that's the version that works without a signal.

Once it's on your phone

What it does

  • 1Pick your name once, and the whole app becomes yours.
  • 2Your next round is pinned at the top, with a live countdown.
  • 3See who you're playing with, and everyone's course handicap.
  • 4Full scorecards, with the holes where you get a stroke marked.
  • 5Tap any course for directions.
  • 6Track your winnings against your buy-in for the week.
Works with no signal once you've opened it — handy at Black Lake.
Two things worth knowing

Good to know

  • ·Tap your name at the top of the app to switch to someone else's schedule.
  • ·Sunday's groups get sorted out when everyone arrives — that one's normal, not a bug.
  • ·Course directions need a signal — they open Maps. Everything else works offline.
